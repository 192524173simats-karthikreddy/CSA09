1. Aim

To design a hospital emergency triage system using Java PriorityQueue. Patients with higher medical severity must be treated first, and patients with equal severity must be treated according to their arrival order.

2. Short Notes
Queue
- Follows FIFO (First In, First Out).
- The first patient who arrives is treated first.
- It cannot directly prioritize patients based on medical severity.
PriorityQueue
- Stores elements according to priority.
- Patients with higher priority are removed first.
- Uses a Comparator to define priority rules.
Comparator
- Compares two patients.
- Lower severity number gets higher priority.
- Earlier arrival gets priority when severity is equal.
Arrival Counter
- Maintains the order in which patients arrive.
- Ensures fairness among patients with equal severity.

3. Design and Working
The hospital uses severity levels from 1 to 5.

Severity	Meaning		Priority
1	        Critical	Highest
2	        Very serious	High
3		Moderate	Medium
4		Minor		Low
5		Least serious	Lowest
